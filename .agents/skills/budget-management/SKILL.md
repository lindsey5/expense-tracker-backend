# Budget management

Use for creating, changing, or reporting budgets in an application that implements them.

- Discover budget scope, period, category or account relationships, uniqueness rules, and how spending is attributed.
- Keep spending filters and period boundaries aligned with the transactions they summarize. Preserve the product's actual thresholds and treatment of overspending; do not invent rollovers or caps.
- Test duplicate allocation, empty periods, boundary dates, threshold transitions, and ownership where applicable. Use financial-calculations for shared arithmetic.
